SAMS — Hero Screen: 4 Design Directions

Student Attendance Management System (CS402.3 Computer Graphics & Visualization, NSBM). Mobile hero screen for Nadeesha, a non-technical admin assistant: upload Signing Sheet photo + Info File, tap Process, watch the image-processing pipeline, then review per-student results with one-tap resolution of Ambiguous rows. All directions: light mode, single column, 375px phone width, colour-blind-safe statuses (icon + text label on every status), Streamlit-achievable layouts.

directions-1.html · 2026-07-10 · each ambiguous row shows its one-tap "Present | Absent" resolution state variant

Direction 1

Quiet Clerk

Calm, minimal, and deliberately unremarkable: one soft neutral surface per task, generous whitespace, and friendly plain-language microcopy so Nadeesha never feels she is operating "software". The pipeline is a modest checklist — reassurance, not spectacle — and the single Ambiguous card is the only thing on the screen allowed to raise its hand.

Direction 2

Lab Instrument

A precision tool that shows its work: monospace metadata, hairline rules, and the seven pipeline stages promoted to first-class content — each with its own thumbnail, stage number, and timing readout. Terse, confident microcopy treats Nadeesha as an operator, and the results table reads like an instrument log with the Ambiguous record flagged for resolution.

Direction 3

Campus Warm

Friendly and human for a non-technical user: warm paper tones, fully rounded cards and pill buttons, initials avatars, and encouraging microcopy that celebrates progress ("All done — 1 student needs your eye"). Every step is phrased as something SAMS did for Nadeesha, and the Ambiguous card asks for help politely with two big, obvious pill buttons.

Direction 4

Demo Sharp

Built to impress in the viva: a strong navy brand header, high-contrast summary stat tiles, a seven-frame pipeline filmstrip with a completion bar, and a crisp, data-dense zebra-striped results table. Every count on screen is a talking point for the demo, and the highlighted Ambiguous row shows the human-in-the-loop resolution in a single glance.
